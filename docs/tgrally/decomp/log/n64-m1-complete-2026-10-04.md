Levers found on it (IDO 5.3 -O2, register allocation of a 1600-insn function):
- **Fewer strength reductions = more s-regs.** The ROM's uopt left loops unreduced; ours reduced them and the SR temporaries crowded out address constants. An `unsigned int n = (r << 2) + i; a[n + 1]` index stops uopt distributing `*size` over the IV sum (no SR), giving the ROM's per-iteration `sll; addu` shape.
- **`r << 2` vs `r * 4`:** the multiply gets SR'd into an IV and the outer test LFTR'd; the shift keeps r as the counter.
- **SR-step reload is a source fingerprint:** a reload of `mem` in a loop step means the source was `base + (mem + 4) * iv`, not a running `y += mem + 4`.
- **Peeled + unrolled loops** (two extra stores then a body unrolled by 4) mean one loop in the source, e.g. one `for (i = 2; i < 16; i++)`, not hand-written [2]/[3] plus a 4..16 loop.
- **`default: break;`** gives PRE an edge block (a load hoisted into the out-of-range path).
- **Empty `if (i || r) {}` at the init/frame join** extends the counters' live ranges and gave the 5th saved register; it costs home stores the ROM lacks, so it's T3-acceptable, not the natural source (T4 open).
- **A5 dead-stack trap:** a frame 8 bytes larger (0x110 vs 0x108) shifted where a callee's uninitialised local read stale stack, so A5 went DIVERGENT while A7 passed. Always check `addiu sp` and fixed slots (sp+0xF3) after adding locals; pad arrays absorb the size.
- **Unicorn tracing:** any extra hook (global UC_HOOK_CODE or unranged MEM_WRITE) broke Sandbox replay; a MEM_WRITE hook ranged on kseg0, kseg1 and physical addresses works.
