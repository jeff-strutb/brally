# Remastered N64 soundtrack

The six pieces are Barry Leitch's Top Gear Rally (N64) music. Every note, volume change and
effect comes from the ROM's modules; the instruments were replaced with the recordings below and
the result mixed in stereo. Each file holds the song once plus its looping section again; the game
plays `[0, loop_end)` and then `[loop_start, loop_end)` repeatedly (`remastered.json`).
